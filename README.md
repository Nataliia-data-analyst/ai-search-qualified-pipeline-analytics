# AI Search → Qualified Pipeline Analytics

### Measuring AI Visibility, Buyer Demand & Revenue Impact

An end-to-end marketing analytics portfolio project designed to measure whether AI search visibility reaches high-intent buyers and contributes to qualified pipeline and business outcomes.

> **Portfolio Project:** All companies, campaigns, opportunities, financial values, and datasets used in this project are fictional and synthetic.

---

## Business Problem

Traditional SEO metrics can indicate strong performance while business outcomes remain weak.

A fictional B2B SaaS company, **NovaFlow**, is experiencing:

- growing organic traffic;
- improving search rankings;
- increasing visibility for informational keywords;
- but limited growth in qualified pipeline.

This creates the core business question:

> **Does AI search visibility reach high-intent buyers and contribute to qualified opportunities, pipeline, and revenue?**

As buyers increasingly use AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot during vendor research, traditional referral and last-click analytics may capture only part of the customer journey.

---

## Project Objective

Build an end-to-end measurement framework connecting:

**AI Visibility → Answer Accuracy → Buyer Demand → Website Acquisition → Qualified Opportunities → Pipeline → Revenue**

---

## Research Questions

1. How often does NovaFlow appear in AI responses for high-intent buyer prompts?
2. How accurately do AI assistants describe the company?
3. Does AI visibility generate identifiable website traffic?
4. Does AI referral traffic convert better than traditional organic traffic?
5. Does branded search demand increase alongside AI visibility?
6. Do AI-influenced buyers become qualified opportunities and contribute to pipeline?
7. How does paid AI advertising compare with organic AI visibility in terms of business impact?
